About the Model

Architecture

Fine-tuned RoBERTa-base (12 layers, 768 hidden dims, ~125 M parameters) for 3-class sentiment: negative, neutral, positive.

Training

  • Base: roberta-base from Hugging Face
  • Fine-tuned on labelled customer reviews
  • Inference: truncated to 128 tokens

Known Limitations

  • Neutral is hardest — mixed reviews often get mis-classified
  • Sarcasm is missed — "Oh great, another broken charger" may read as positive
  • Short reviews — single words or emojis give the model little context
  • 128-token cap — very long reviews are silently truncated
